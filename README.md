# ⚡ Electric Vehicle (EV) Market Analysis — India Entry Strategy for AtliQ Motors

> *"AtliQ Motors holds 25% market share in North America. In India? Less than 2%. This analysis tells them exactly where to go, when to launch, and who to beat."*

---

## 📌 The Business Problem

**AtliQ Motors** is a U.S.-based EV manufacturer with a strong foothold in North America. The company's next target: **India** — one of the fastest-growing EV markets in the world.

But India is not North America. The market is fragmented, competition is fierce, and existing players have already dug in deep.

**Bruce Haryali**, Head of AtliQ Motors India, needed data-backed answers to six strategic questions before committing to an India launch:

| # | Question |
|---|---|
| 1 | Who are the competitors we're up against? |
| 2 | Which state and month should we launch in? |
| 3 | Which states should we avoid entirely? |
| 4 | Delhi vs Karnataka — which is the better bet? |
| 5 | What's the revenue and sales growth trajectory? |
| 6 | What will the market look like by 2030? |

---

## 🎯 Project Goal

Conduct a full market intelligence study of the Indian EV landscape (FY 2022–2024) using SQL data wrangling and Tableau visualisations — and deliver **three clear, prioritised recommendations** for AtliQ Motors' India entry.

## 🔍 Key Findings

### 🏍️ 2-Wheeler Market — Who Dominates?

- **OLA Electric** holds the top position by a massive margin (3.26M units in FY2023)
- **TVS** is a steady #2
- **Bajaj** displaced Ather to claim #3 in FY2024 — a shift worth watching
- 2-Wheeler EV sales grew **188%** from FY2022 → FY2023, approaching **1 million units** by FY2024

### 🚗 4-Wheeler Market — Tata's India

- **Tata Motors** sells almost **3× more** than the other top 4 manufacturers combined
- **BYD India** has the highest CAGR at **567%** (FY2022–2024) — a rising threat
- Revenue from 4-wheelers crossed **$1.5 billion** in FY2024

### 🗺️ Where to Launch — 2-Wheelers

| State | Penetration Rate (FY2024) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Ladakh | 26.9% | Too small a market |
| **Goa** | **24%** | ✅ **Best launch state** — high penetration, tourist + local demand, strong infrastructure |
| Kerala | 16.4% | Strong secondary market |
| Karnataka | 13.7% | Emerging, growing fast |
| Maharashtra | 12.3% | Large market, more competition |

### 🗺️ Where to Launch — 4-Wheelers

| State | Penetration Rate | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Kerala | 8.7% | High adoption |
| Chandigarh | 7.2% | Small market |
| Goa | 5.3% | Solid dual-market |
| **Delhi** | **5.1%** | ✅ Large market + EV-friendly policies |
| **Karnataka** | **4.23%** | ✅ Emerging market, less competition, strong govt incentives |

### ❌ Where NOT to Launch

**Sikkim** — zero EV penetration in both FY2022 and FY2024. Small market, challenging geography, no infrastructure. Avoid.

### 🆚 Delhi vs Karnataka — The Head-to-Head

Karnataka wins for **manufacturers**, despite lower sales volume:

| Factor | Delhi | Karnataka |
|---|---|---|
| EV Sales (FY2024) | 47K | 161K |
| Penetration Rate | 7.7% | 10.2% |
| Capital Subsidy | — | **15% for EV manufacturers** |
| Tax Rebates | — | **SGST refund on EV sales** |
| Land Concessions | — | **Concessional land for EV facilities** |
| Interest-Free Loans | — | **For charging infrastructure** |
| Electricity Tariff | — | **Reduced for EV production** |

### 📅 Best Month to Launch

**March** — peak sales month with **292K vehicles sold**, nearly 2× the average of off-peak months (June: 104K). Time the launch for Q4 of the fiscal year.

### 🔋 Charging Infrastructure — A Critical Gap

Even in the top 5 states, charging infrastructure is dangerously thin:

| State | EV Sales | Charging Stations | Stations per 1,000 EVs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maharashtra | 396K | 3,079 | 8 |
| Karnataka | 313K | 1,041 | 3 |
| Tamil Nadu | 200K | 643 | 3 |
| Gujarat | 181K | 476 | 3 |
| Rajasthan | 150K | 500 | 3 |

**Only 3–8 charging stations per 1,000 EVs.** This is both a market gap and a strategic opportunity.

### 📈 Projected Market by 2030

Maharashtra, Kerala, Gujarat, and Karnataka are projected to hit **8–13 million EV sales by 2030** — the highest growth corridor in India.

---

## 💡 Top 3 Recommendations for AtliQ Motors

| # | Recommendation | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Launch 2-Wheelers in Goa first** | 24% penetration rate + tourist demand + strong infrastructure = fastest path to market presence |
| 2 | **Set up 4-Wheeler operations in Karnataka** | 10.2% penetration rate + 15% capital subsidy + tax rebates + reduced electricity tariffs = best economics for a manufacturer |
| 3 | **Invest in charging infrastructure** | Only 3 stations per 1,000 EVs in most states. Partner with state governments to build charging networks — this creates a moat and accelerates adoption |

**Timing:** Target a **March launch** to align with peak EV sales season.
